Event Coordination by Eventz By Twinkle

Seamless Execution. Exceptional Attention to Detail.

Our coordination service ensures that every detail of your event is expertly managed from setup to completion. We work closely with vendors, manage timelines, oversee logistics, coordinate event flow, and handle on-the-day details so you can be fully present.

This package is designed for clients who have already planned their wedding and are looking for a professional team to oversee the details and ensure a seamless flow throughout the event.

Minimum coordination requirement: every event requires at least four coordinators consisting of a bride's assistant, groom's assistant, event grounds coordinator, and the overall team lead.

What the Coordination Team Handles

  • Vendor communication and timing
  • Venue setup supervision
  • Timeline and logistics management
  • Guest and flow coordination
  • Ceremony and reception transitions
  • Final send-off and wrap-up support

Terms and Conditions

Read Carefully Before Booking

  1. 60% non-refundable down payment is required for booking. The balance must be paid not less than a week to the event.
  2. Bookings of vendors start when both parties have agreed on a specific vendor.
  3. Service charge remains the same until there is a change in the event.
  4. Cost of accommodation and transportation outside Accra will be taken care of by the client at an agreed fee.
  5. Flight costs will be covered by the client for the team lead and her assistants for locations such as Kumasi, Takoradi, Sunyani, and Tamale.
  6. If your event goes more than 2 days, further discussions will be made to tweak the costing.
  7. Any other event which is not an engagement or a wedding will be discussed separately.
  8. We do not encourage excessive inputs from friends and family or a do-it-yourself approach, though we can recommend other event professionals where needed.
  9. Client agrees to allow Eventz By Twinkle to use pictures and videos for promotional use on our social media platforms, websites and other promotional tools. However, an NDA will be signed if the client opts for a PRIVATE EVENT.
  10. Prices for additional services are as follows; Ushers – 300gh per usher, Waiter – 300gh per waiter. Security-700gh per personnel.
  11. A contract will be signed by both parties after booking.
